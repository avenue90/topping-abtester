Hardware diagnostics

      
Close the Topping Home device session, then read your DX1 II firmware and state. This sends read requests only and closes the connection afterward. A timeout may mean the firmware needs the vendor’s connection handshake; it does not mean the DAC is faulty.

Pre-gain compensation matches the unboosted parts of the signal mathematically. EQ changes tonal balance, so use listening trim for perceived-level comparison. Live mode leaves headphone gain unchanged. This independent project is not affiliated with Topping. Device communication stays in your browser; this site has no account or telemetry.
